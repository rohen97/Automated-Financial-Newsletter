Africa's Public Debt Amid Global Headwinds: Balancing Resilience and Vulnerabilities

RATESINFLATIONFXENERGYGEOPOLITICS
The view

The conflict in the Middle East has exacerbated fiscal pressures in Africa, raising energy and fertiliser prices while slowing growth. Despite a shift towards domestic currency debt that reduces exchange rate risk, the reliance on short maturities and higher rates has heightened debt service costs and rollover risks. Enhanced government financing through banks has implications for credit availability and financial stability, necessitating efforts to diversify investor bases and strengthen market structures.
