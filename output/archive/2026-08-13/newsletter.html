Africa's Public Debt Amid Global Headwinds: Balancing Resilience and Vulnerabilities

The ongoing conflict in the Middle East is driving up energy and fertilizer prices, exacerbating growth challenges and fiscal pressures across Africa. Elevated public debt levels coupled with a shift towards domestic currency debt have mitigated exchange rate risks but have resulted in higher debt service costs and increased rollover risks. The heavy reliance on banks and central banks for financing can hinder private credit availability and pose risks to financial stability, underscoring the need for deeper markets and diversified investor bases.
