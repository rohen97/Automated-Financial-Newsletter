Africa's Public Debt Amid Global Headwinds: Balancing Resilience and Vulnerabilities

The ongoing Middle East conflict is pushing energy and fertilizer prices upward, straining fiscal stability across Africa. With public debt and debt service burdens elevated, the shift towards domestic currency debt has mitigated exchange rate risks but escalated debt servicing costs and rollover risks. A reliance on banks for government financing exacerbates the sovereign-bank nexus, potentially sidelining private credit and threatening financial stability. Developing deeper markets and a diversified investor base is critical for addressing these challenges.
